# Welcome to Richie Finance

PDAI deserves more places to go, more liquidity to move through, and more yield opportunities for holders.

**Richie Finance is built as the liquidity and yield layer for the PDAI community on PulseChain.** Our goal is to help PDAI holders deepen liquidity, earn sustainable yield on their PDAI holdings, and expand PDAI's utility through an ecosystem designed around productive capital.

At the center of the protocol are two tokens:

* **$CASH** is the protocol's algorithmic token, targeting a market price of **1 PDAI per CASH** through supply expansion and contraction.
* **$RICH** is the fixed supply share token of the protocol, it represents protocol ownership and reflects confidence in the peg and in the treasury behind it. RICH gives stakers sole rights to earn newly minted CASH.

Our approach to maintaining a strong link between CASH and PDAI starts with the PSM (Peg Stability Module). The PSM is funded through a small fee that's paid when claiming LP farming rewards in The Marina, this fee is paid in PLS and is always less than the value of your claimable rewards. 

The PSM fee goes to funding the treasury reserves which is built up of a variety of Pulsechain blue chip assets. The treasury will actively manage these assets and utilize them in yielding opportunities on chain in order to maximize income growth and benefit the Pulsechain ecosystem, this then allows CASH to develop a claimable backing and support its peg to PDAI.

**Build liquidity. Grow productive capital. Give the peg more muscle.**

## PDAI moves freely

PDAI is welcome aboard, and free to come and go as it pleases. Your PDAI is forever yours and is never locked. The protocol begins with a Genesis launch to distribute the initial supply of CASH tokens over a 3 day period. To participate in the genesis users can stake a variety of Pulsechain assets including PDAI, PLS, HEX, PLSX, ICHIDAI, Atropa and DAI. During genesis you are free to deposit/withdraw and claim your rewards at any time. 

Genesis deposits have a 1% fee, which is used to build up the initial treasury that maintains the CASH to PDAI peg. 

## The Richie Finance approach

RICH liquidity incentives help attract participation and build deep PDAI liquidity. The Peg Stability Module turns reward claims into a source of treasury funding. Treasury activity seeks to develop further revenue streams, while treasury rebates offer another way to acquire assets and liquidity to help bolster CASH backing and protocol revenue.

The ambition is a protocol with more tools to work with across market cycles and benefit the Pulsechain ecosystem. Those tools carry risk, and their effectiveness depends on actual revenue, accessible capital, and market conditions.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**A target peg is not a guarantee.** CASH is not a fully collateral-backed stablecoin. CASH can trade well above or below its target based on market demand.
{% endhint %}
