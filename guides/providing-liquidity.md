# Providing liquidity

Providing liquidity is the way to contribute to Pulsechain's PDAI market depth while earning RICH shares. CASH/PDAI and RICH/PLS LPs can both be staked in The Marina to bolster liquidity of these tokens and earn RICH shares for doing so.

## The process

1. Create LP tokens by supplying equal amounts (in price) of the two tokens in your chosen LP token on Pulsex V2 or by zapping into an LP token by supplying only one side of that LP token. Zapping can be done from The Marina page on our site.
2. Deposit the LP token into the relevant pool on The Marina and immediately begin earning RICH shares. Deposits are not taxed and can be unstaked at anytime. Holding LP tokens in your wallet does not earn RICH shares, they must be staked in The Marina.
3. To claim RICH share rewards click claim on the pool in The Marina, ensure you have enough PLS in your wallet to cover the PSM fee when claiming.

See [liquidity farms](../protocol/liquidity-farms.md) and [claiming rewards](claiming-rewards.md).
