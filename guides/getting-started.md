# Getting started

There are several ways you can participate in the Richie Finance ecosystem

This includes: staking CASH/PDAI or RICH/PLS LP tokens in The Marina to earn RICH shares, staking RICH in The Yacht Club to earn new mints of CASH every epoch, purchasing discounted RICH shares through the rebate center or redeeming CASH for PDAI backing when below peg. 

Each part has its own benefits and reward structure and all together form the Richie Finance ecosystem that aims to be the liquidity and yield layer for PDAI.


## Prepare your wallet

Use a wallet that supports PulseChain and holds enough PLS for network gas. RICH farm reward claims require a PSM fee paid in PLS, so plan for that cost separately and have enough PLS gas in your wallet.

## Choose a route

| Goal | Intended route | Read first |
